I'm an economist graduated from the [University of Costa Rica](https://www.ucr.ac.cr/). I'm interested in econometrics, data science, data journalism and finance.

I'm passionate about machine learning and artificial intelligence, especially their impact on the labor market and the economy. I believe understanding these technologies is key to navigating the transformations to come.

I currently work as a research assistant at [Doble Check](https://doblecheck.ucr.ac.cr/), the University of Costa Rica's fact-checking project, where I verify public claims and analyze government finances using official data.

Previously, I interned at [CINDE](https://www.cinde.org/) in the Research, Monitoring & Evaluation area, and spent an exchange semester at [Universidad Carlos III de Madrid](https://www.uc3m.es/), where I earned honors in Machine Learning. 

When I'm not coding or crunching data, I enjoy reading about economic history and watching football. I dream of applying my skills to improve Costa Rica's public policies.

If you'd like to collaborate on a project or just chat, [drop me a line](mailto:mrojas@ucr.ac.cr). I'm always open to new ideas.
